WHO WE ARE

A decade of celebrating the warmth, soul, and timeless crackle of vinyl culture. Our mission is to preserve the analog experience and provide a high-fidelity sanctuary for selectors and deep listeners alike. Join us as we explore the deeper grooves and rarest pressings in an intimate, auditory-focused environment.

ESTABLISHED 2014

Born from a Crate

Vinyl Vibes started as a small pop-up stall in the local night market. What began as a personal collection of rare disco and soul records soon grew into a community hub for audiophiles and casual listeners alike.

In 2020, we realized that music sounds better when shared. We integrated our record store with a high-fidelity listening bar, creating a space where the quality of the drink matches the quality of the sound.

Purely Analog

We believe in the depth and texture of analog sound. No streaming, no digital filters—just raw signal from the stylus to the speaker.

Curation First

We don't stock everything. We stock the best. Every record in our bins is verified for quality and artistic merit by our curator team.

Community Driven

Music is a universal language. Our space is designed to encourage conversation, sharing, and discovery among strangers.
